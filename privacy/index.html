Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-24.

AMP Optics, LLC (“AMP Optics,” “we,” “us”) operates this website. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to submit through the contact form: your name, email address, company (optional), and the content of your message.

How it is used

Information submitted through the contact form is delivered directly to AMP Optics’ email inbox via a third-party form-delivery service, Web3Forms, solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. It is not added to a mailing list, used for marketing, or used for any purpose beyond replying to you.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, other than the form-delivery service described above, which is necessary to transmit your message to us.

Cookies & tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind. The only third-party requests made by this site are to Google Fonts, to load the typefaces used on the page; no tracking cookie is set by this request.

Data retention

Messages submitted through the contact form are retained in our email inbox for as long as reasonably necessary to address your inquiry, consistent with normal business record-keeping.

Your choices

Because this site does not track visitors or use cookies, there is nothing to opt out of. If you would like a copy of information you submitted through the contact form, or would like it deleted, reach out via LinkedIn or the contact form itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
