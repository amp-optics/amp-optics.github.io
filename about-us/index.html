About AMP Optics

Fifteen years of moving optical systems from “blue-sky” to reality.

AMP Optics, LLC was founded in 2010 to expedite conception, development and production of complex optical systems. AMP Optics specializes in system development through efficient optical design and analysis to move optical systems from “Blue-Sky” to reality. With experience at the leading edge of optical technology, AMP has created solutions in free-space communication, medical, aerospace, defense, lithography, imaging, hyperspectral, polarimetric and other areas.

AMP Optics helps customers on projects from simple optical design and analysis services to complete project management, supply chain setup and system integration. AMP utilizes in-house expertise and coordinates sourcing of external expertise from our extensive network of partners to provide your project the best solution.

With expertise in all areas of optical system development — design, tolerance analysis, STOP analysis, testing, alignment — and strong relationships with optical coating and manufacturing suppliers, AMP Optics optimizes customer solutions for performance, price and schedule.

— Gene Cross, Lockheed Martin Advanced Technology Center

The Principal

AMP Optics was founded by Adam Phenis, an optical engineering consultant with more than two decades of experience taking optical systems from conception through production and delivery. He is currently supporting customers as the Lead Optical Engineer on multiple space-based visible and infrared optical payloads and on material processing with high-power lasers. His systems have operated everywhere from the lab to space, airborne, automotive and weapon-mounted environments.

Before founding AMP Optics, Mr. Phenis held senior optical engineering roles at Lockheed Martin Advanced Technology Center — where he took the first-ever polarimetric images of launch-phase rockets, including the Space Shuttle — AOptix Technologies, B.E. Meyers, Leidos (formerly SAIC) and Cymer, an ASML company, where he supported EUV lithography development.

Mr. Phenis chairs the joint SPIE / Optics and Electro-Optics Standards Council (OEOSC) Infrared Materials Standards Working Group, serves as Chair of OEOSC, previously served as convenor of the ISO working group on optical coating standards, and co-authored the “Infrared Optical Systems” chapter of the SPIE Press book Review of Optical Manufacturing 2000 to 2020.

He holds an M.S. in Optical Sciences from the University of Arizona, where his master’s thesis on wavefront-sensing interferometers was advised by Professor Jim Wyant, and a B.S. in Optical Science and Engineering from the University of California, Davis.
