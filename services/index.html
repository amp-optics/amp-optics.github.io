Services

Not sure what your project needs? That's normal — scoping the problem is where we start. AMP Optics is here to bring your ‘blue-sky’ concepts to reality.

Optical Design

Imaging and non-imaging system design from first-order layout through production-ready prescriptions — including coherent propagation and specialized infrared systems, with deep knowledge of infrared materials. Designs are engineered for manufacturability from the start, not fixed after the fact.

Tools: CODE V · Ansys Zemax OpticStudio · FRED

Engineering Analysis

Analysis that predicts how the as-built system will actually perform: tolerance analysis to set achievable specs, stray light analysis and baffle design, structural-thermal-optical performance (STOP) analysis for harsh environments, and polarization analysis for demanding radiometric systems.

Tools: CODE V · Ansys Zemax OpticStudio · FRED · MATLAB · Mathcad

Optomechanical & Mechanical Design

Mounts, housings, baffles and structures designed alongside the optics — plus creation and review of ISO 10110 optical drawings that any supplier can build from.

Tools: SolidWorks

Manufacturing & Supply Chain Support

Early consultation with optical coating suppliers and manufacturers to ensure the system is manufacturable with ease, supplier selection and management, and full project management through system integration, testing and validation.

Built on long-standing relationships with prominent optics suppliers
